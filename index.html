ABOUT THE CENTER

A healthier Barangay Pag-Asa starts here.

The Pag-Asa Healthcare Center is committed to providing accessible, organized, and responsive healthcare services to the residents of Barangay Pag-Asa, Binangonan, Rizal.

This web-based system helps residents find services, request appointments, and receive important health announcements while helping healthcare staff manage daily services more efficiently.
